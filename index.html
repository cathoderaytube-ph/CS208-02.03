Benguet, Philippines

Location

Benguet is located 5 hours north of Manila, the capital city of the Philippines.

About

Elevation

According to topographic-map.com, Benguet has an average elevation of 883m(2,897ft) with a minimum elevation of -1m(-3ft) and a maximum elevation of 2,841m(9,606ft).

Languages

The villages in Benguet speak their own dinstinct languages in addition to 5 dominant languages: Ilocano, Tagalog, English, Kankanaey, and Ibaloi.

Population

According to the Philippine Statistics Authority in 2024, the province has a population of 473,190.

Things To Do

  • Hiking
  • Trying local dishes
  • Strawberry picking
  • Connecting with the locals
